## Product Context

Dispatch is an AI-powered news digest that curates the world's most important stories into a single, scannable daily briefing. It's built for professionals, founders, and curious minds who need to stay informed without drowning in noise. Each edition distills dozens of sources into a sharp, editorially-toned summary — giving readers the signal, not the static.

The product lives on the web. Its reading experience is the core: a clean, newspaper-inspired layout with strong typographic hierarchy that makes scanning effortless. Every design decision serves readability and trust. The serif-driven aesthetic borrows from print journalism's authority while staying unmistakably digital — fast, interactive, and alive.

Key flows:

1. **Daily digest view** — The landing experience. A dated edition with a lead story, sectioned headlines, and brief summaries. Scrollable, scannable, authoritative.
2. **Topic/category drill-down** — Readers tap a section (World, Tech, Markets, Culture) to see deeper coverage with source links and AI commentary.
3. **Archive/history** — Browse past editions by date, search by keyword, or revisit saved stories.

## Content Fundamentals

**Voice:** Direct and confident, with a playful undercurrent. The product speaks like a sharp editor who genuinely enjoys the news — never dry, never breathless. Wry observations are welcome; hyperbole is not.

**Casing:** Title Case for section headings and navigation labels. Sentence case for summaries and body text. ALL CAPS with wide letter-spacing for metadata labels (DATE, SECTION, SOURCE).

**Person:** Third-person editorial ("Today's briefing covers…"). First-person reserved for the publisher's note or brand voice ("Dispatch by Aaron Mompié").

**Emoji:** Not used in editorial content. Permitted sparingly in UI chrome (e.g., a small indicator dot, not a full emoji).

**Tone examples:**

> "Markets rallied on the back of a cooler-than-expected jobs report — because nothing says optimism like signs of a slowing economy."
>
> "Three stories you need today. No filler, no hot takes — just the signal."
>
> "Your morning briefing is ready. 7 stories, 4 minutes."

**Numbers and labels:** 12h time for timestamps. Dates as "Apr 20, 2026" (short month, no ordinal). Source counts as digits. Reading time as "X min read".

**Handwritten accents:** Not used. The brand is typographically disciplined — serif for authority, sans for clarity.

## Visual Foundations

**Core aesthetic:** Cool-professional editorial with a playful voice. Sharp angles, strong serifs, generous whitespace. Think broadsheet meets terminal — authoritative but never stuffy.

**Color:** See `colors_and_type.css` for tokens. Summary:

- **Surfaces** Cool neutral backgrounds, 6 steps from near-white to deep charcoal — `#f8f8f6` to `#1a1a1e`
- **Text** Ink-dark primary to muted gray — `#101218` to `#8a8f99`
- **Primary accent** A warm, assertive orange — `#f26522` (inherited from Aaron Mompié's brand)
- **Supporting colors** Teal `#1a9a8a`, Crimson `#c4322e`, Gold `#c9941a`, Violet `#6b4c9a`
- **Semantic** Green success, amber warning, red error, blue info
- **No** Pure black (#000), pure white (#fff), or generic SaaS blue

**Type:**

- **Instrument Serif** (italic preferred) — Display headings, section titles, the logo wordmark. Conveys editorial authority and typographic personality.
- **Archivo** — Body text, UI labels, metadata. Clean grotesque with excellent legibility at small sizes. Supports the full weight range (100–900).
- **JetBrains Mono** — Timestamps, source counts, data values. Adds a "newsroom terminal" feel.

**Spacing:** 4px grid. Generous whitespace between sections (32–64px). Compact within cards and lists (8–16px gutters).

**Backgrounds:** Flat, slightly warm off-whites. No gradients on backgrounds. Subtle surface elevation through shadows and borders, not color shifts.

**Patterns / textures:** None. Clean, editorial flatness. Borders and rules do the work that textures would.

**Animation:**

- Easing: `cubic-bezier(0.22, 0.61, 0.36, 1)` (default — smooth deceleration)
- Spring: `cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)` (for interactive feedback)
- Durations: 100ms (instant), 150ms (fast), 220ms (normal), 350ms (slow)
- Snappy and minimal — no decorative motion, only functional transitions

**Hover:** Subtle lift (translateY -1px) on cards and buttons. Border color intensifies. Background shifts one surface step darker.

**Press:** Scale 0.98 on buttons. Background darkens. No bounces.

**Focus:** 2px solid `--primary-2` outline, 2px offset. Rounded corners on the outline at 4px.

**Borders:** 1px solid for default elements. `--line-1` for subtle dividers, `--line-2` for default borders, `--line-3` for active/focus states. No dashed or dotted borders.

**Shadows:** Cool-neutral shadows with slight blue undertone. 5-step scale from xs to xl. Inset shadow for pressed/active states.

**Transparency / blur:** Backdrop blur (12px) on sticky navigation. No other blur effects. Semi-transparent overlays for modals (rgba 0.4).

**Imagery tone:** Clean, high-contrast. No grain or vintage treatment. News images displayed at full fidelity. AI-generated illustrations use the brand palette.

**Corner radii:** Sharp/angular language dominates. Small radii only where needed for modern feel: 2px (subtle), 4px (buttons/inputs), 6px (cards), 8px (modals). No pill shapes except for tags/badges (9999px).

**Cards:** Flat surface with 1px `--line-1` border, 6px radius, `--sh-sm` shadow. Padding 20px internal. Hover: border → `--line-2`, shadow → `--sh-md`, translateY -1px.

**Fixed elements:** Sticky navigation bar (top, backdrop blur). Toast notifications (bottom-right). No floating action buttons.

**Layout rules:** Max-width 72rem (1152px). Content area max 70ch for reading. 2rem gutters on mobile, scaling up. 12-column grid for complex layouts, simple flexbox for editorial pages. Breakpoints: 760px (mobile), 1024px (tablet), 1280px (desktop).

## Iconography

**System:** Lucide (clean outlines, 1.5px stroke)

- **Stroke weight:** 1.5px
- **Size:** 16px (inline), 20px (default), 24px (prominent)
- **Color:** `--text-1` default, `--primary-2` active, `--text-3` disabled
- **Never:** Filled icons, emoji as icons, decorative icon clusters

**Icons in use:**

- `newspaper` — Edition/digest icon
- `clock` — Timestamp, reading time
- `bookmark` — Save story
- `share-2` — Share story
- `chevron-right` — Section drill-down
- `arrow-up-right` — External links
- `search` — Archive search
- `calendar` — Date picker
- `bell` — Notifications
- `settings` — Preferences
- `menu` — Mobile navigation
- `x` — Close/dismiss
- `sun` / `moon` — Theme toggle (future)
- `filter` — Category filter

**Unicode glyphs:**

- `—` (em-dash) — Used in editorial text for parenthetical breaks
- `→` (right arrow, `&#8594;`) — Link indicators
- `↗` (up-right arrow, `&#8599;`) — External links
- `·` (middle dot) — Inline separators in metadata rows

**Logo mark:** "Dispatch" set in Instrument Serif Italic. The "D" may be used as a standalone mark. Warm orange accent bar (2px × 24px) beneath the logo, left-aligned.

**SVG decor:** No hand-drawn elements. Editorial rules (horizontal lines) replace decorative SVGs.

## How to Use This System

Read `SKILL.md` first, then `colors_and_type.css`, then explore the preview cards and UI kits. Use CSS variables — never hard-code hex values. When in doubt, err toward the editorial aesthetic: more whitespace, stronger type hierarchy, sharper edges.
