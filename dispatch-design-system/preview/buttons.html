Button Guidelines
Primary: Main actions — Submit, Save, Continue. Use the editorial blue.
Secondary: Alternative actions — Cancel, Back, Dismiss. Subtle background.
Accent: Conversion CTAs — Subscribe, Sign up. Reserved for revenue actions.
Ghost: Low-emphasis actions — Skip, Close. Transparent with hover state.
Text: Inline links — Read more, View all. Underline on hover.
Radius: 8px for all buttons. No full-width pills except badges.
Shadow: sm on rest, md on hover, xs on press.
