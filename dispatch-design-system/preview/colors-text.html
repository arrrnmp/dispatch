Text Colors — Hierarchy
text-0 #0a0c10
Primary text for headings, body content, and UI elements. Near-black with cool undertone.
text-1 #3d4249
Secondary text for paragraphs and descriptions. Slightly muted for comfortable reading.
text-2 #5b6470
Tertiary text for captions, metadata, and de-emphasized content. Cool gray tone.
text-3 #8a9199
Placeholder text, disabled states, and very subtle labels. Reserved for non-interactive elements.
